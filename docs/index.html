does it work? honest section

  • it's a prompt + a thin CLI. it's as good as the model you point it at.
  • no benchmarks yet. we're not making up a number for a banner.
  • summaries are lossy. contracts, medical, money: read the real thing.
  • it flags "looks made up". it doesn't fact-check. it doesn't browse.
  • your text goes to the model provider you choose. don't pipe in secrets.

faq

is this just "summarize"?

a summary says what's there. bruh also says what they want from you, what's padding, and what smells made up. also it says "bruh."

will it hurt my coworker's feelings?

roast mode roasts the slop, never the person. their AI has it coming.

is it the reverse of caveman?

spiritually. caveman makes the AI say less. bruh helps with what was already said. use both. take a nap.

is it free?

MIT licensed, free forever. you bring your own API key.

why are the error messages rude?

not rude. honest. input is empty, bruh.
